The name is Amir!” says the tall meerkat. “Heya! I’m Samir!” says the short one. “We are workin mighty hard trying to break through this wall, but it's tough as a grandma’s toenail!” Amir has an idea. “You look like a smart fella. Any ideas?” Alfie realizes that maybe the other side of the wall is a path that leads to the outside! With this in mind, he decides to help. Who knows what awaits them on the other side…
