“What is this?” says Alfie. “What, that right there is one of my rarest items!” says Roscoe. “They call it a Train Thicket. You can use it to ride a giant caterpillar that takes you all over the land.” Alfie’s mind is blown! He wants to see the caterpillar for himself, but unfortunately, he realizes he would have to be above ground to do so. He puts the ticket back sadly.
