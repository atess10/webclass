Alfie's eye catches a bag of fertilizer at the end of the table. “This! Back home, all the other flowers talk about how fertilizer helps you grow big and strong!” says Aflie excitedly. He purchased the bag from Roscoe with lightning speed. He unseals the bag and gobbles up the whole thing like a starving wolf. Then he waits, and he waits, and he waits. But he doesn’t grow. “Fertilizer takes quite a while, my friend. You just gonna have to wait and see, I am sorry to say.” Alfie sulks for a good moment. Another failed attempt to escape. Before long, though, he shakes it off and bids Roscoe farewell. On to another tunnel!
