“Tell me about your day. That is it, that is all I want to know.” Alfie was certainly confused, but he decided to humor his new human companion. He told him about the flower patch that had miraculously grown underground despite all odds. The funny mole merchant with his ant steed and a wide range of strange items for sale. Then the rambunctious meerkat twins had an exciting dig for buried treasure. Alfie went into detail about all the mistakes he made traveling around and meeting his new friends, and how exciting it felt whenever he turned a corner on something new. Before he knew it, Alfie had a smile on his face recounting his day. The old man looked at Alfie and smiled back. “My my my what an adventure. Sounds like you have all kinds of stories to tell about living down here. Say, for someone who is bored, you sure had an exciting day!”
