Alfie, with much enthusiasm, waddled over to some of the fishing supplies the old man had on the ground. He lifts one of them up and brings it over to the fisherman. “Here! Will this help you find the petalfish faster?” The old man listens intently to what Alfie is saying, but responds earnestly. “Now, why would I want to do that?” Alfie puts his leaves on his hips and cocks his head. “Don’t you want to leave? I thought your goal was to find this fish and then get back aboveground. Where all the exciting stuff is happening!” The old man laughs a good, hearty laugh. “Exciting? Well, if aboveground was so exciting, why would I come down here? This here body of water is the only one in the world where you can find a petalfish.” Alfie frowns. “Well, that may be good for you, but it isn’t for me. I want to leave and see the world. I want to grow and grow and feel the whole sun on my face.” The old man looked at Alfie and could see how much this weighed on his little heart. “My friend, do you know why the petalfish is so rare? It isn’t because it is underground. It is because it takes patience. Sometimes days, sometimes months. One can never really know.” Alfie slumped his posture. “How is that supposed to help me?” The fisherman smiled. “Well, it means we have time to talk. I can tell you anything about aboveground that you want. But I need you to do one thing for me.” Alfie put his leaf to his chin in response. What could this be?
