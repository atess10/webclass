A disappointed Alfie walked down another tunnel and heard the sound of water echoing soothingly throughout the area. When he turned the corner, he could not believe what he saw. An old underground lake with a rickety dock that had a human sitting on it. A human! Alfie had not heard much about humans but knew that humans lived in a big, exciting world above the ground. This was his chance to leave! “Hello, mister human!” said Alfie excitedly. “What are you doing down here?” The elderly man responded in a calm but delighted tone, “Well, hello, little one. I am what we humans call a fisherman. I have been all over the world catching all sorts of fish. But I came underground in search of the rarest fish of them all. The petalfish!” Alfie wasted no time with his response, “When will you go back above ground!” The old man chuckled. “A curious one, aren’t you. Well, as soon as I catch the petalfish, I will make my departure. I take it you would like to come with?” Alfie could barely contain his excitement. “Absolutely!” Alfie was thrilled. Finally, he was about to get what he always wanted. His ticket out of the boring underground. If he could just somehow help the old man find the petalfish as soon as possible…
