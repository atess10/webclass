“What about this drill?” says Alfie. The drill looks sleek, shiny, and fresh out of the hardware store! Samir lifts it up with expertise and positions it at a very specific point in the rock wall. “Good eye, my flowery friend! I have been eyeing this puppy ever since we bought it from that weird shopkeeper. Here goes nothin!” Samir clicks the switch, and the drill immediately starts ripping through the stone. But very quickly, it makes a horrifying mechanical sound and starts to steam. Then the machine explodes, sending Samir flying through the cave. Amir puts his hands on his hips and stares at the wall with confusion. “What the hell is this damn thing made of?” Back to the drawing board!
