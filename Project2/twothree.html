“What about just using this pickaxe?” says Alfie. Samir and Amir look at each other and shrug. “Maybe we just needed an extra hand. Can’t complain, a solution that doesn’t include blowing us up,” says the tall meerkat. The three get right to work with the pickaxes. They chip and chip and dig and dig all day and all night. So much time passes, they aren’t quite sure if it's been hours, days, or weeks. The wall doesn’t budge. The three lay on the floor out of exhaustion. They have not made a dent. Back to the drawing board!
